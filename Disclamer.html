🖼️ Image Sourcing & Open Licensing

All images stream dynamically via public open-source APIs: Openverse API and Wikimedia Commons API.

TierPix only queries media released under verifiable open licenses (Creative Commons, Public Domain, CC0). Creator attributions and license URLs are linked directly per image.

⚖️ Fair Use & Educational Purpose

TierPix functions as a non-commercial interactive content discovery, image evaluation, and taxonomy research engine.

Thumbnails rendered on tier lists are displayed for transformative evaluation and sorting purposes under Fair Use guidelines.

🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

TierPix does not host, upload, or store image files on external servers. All user ratings and tier chart preferences are stored strictly in your browser's local storage (localStorage).
